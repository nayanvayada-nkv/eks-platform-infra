# SOP-DL-02: Delivery delays (Kestrel Logistics, fictional)

A shipment counts as delayed when it misses the promised delivery date by more than 24 hours.
For delays caused by Kestrel, the customer receives a 10% shipping credit after 48 hours of delay and
a 25% shipping credit after 5 days. No credit is given for delays caused by weather alerts, strikes,
customs holds, or an incorrect address provided by the customer.
Agents should open a trace request in the tracking tool for any delay above 72 hours.

# SOP-CL-04: Claims for damaged parcels (Kestrel Logistics, fictional)

Customers must file a damage claim within 7 calendar days of the delivery date shown in tracking.
Claims after 7 days are rejected unless the parcel was delivered to a neighbour or locker, in which case
the limit is 10 days.

Required evidence: at least two photos of the outer packaging, two photos of the damaged item, the
original invoice, and the tracking number. Agents must not promise a refund before the claims team
approves the claim. Approved claims are paid within 30 days to the original payment method.
High-value claims above INR 50,000 need an inspection by a surveyor before approval.

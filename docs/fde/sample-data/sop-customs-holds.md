# SOP-CU-01: Customs holds on international parcels (Kestrel Logistics, fictional)

When a parcel is held by customs, the receiver must upload a copy of their government ID and the
commercial invoice in the customs portal within 3 working days. Duties and taxes are payable by the
receiver unless the shipment was booked as DDP (Delivered Duty Paid). Agents must never estimate duty
amounts; they share the customs portal link instead. Parcels not cleared within 15 days are returned
to the sender at the sender's cost.
